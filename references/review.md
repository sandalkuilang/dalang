# Fresh-eyes review (Step 7)

Before Gate 4 one reviewer with no build history looks at the film. It only finds problems; `qc.md` Step 7 decides.

## How

1. Produce the sheets: `npx hyperframes snapshot --review --describe false` (scene sheet, a strip at every cut, a 360 px phone sheet) and `node <skill-dir>/scripts/strips.mjs --crops` (10 %, 50 % and 90 % of each storyboard row, one row per shot on `review/strips/strip-NN.jpg`, frame → time → shot in `index.md`, plus `review/strips/crop-NN.jpg`, the subject crop per shot: centre half, or `--crop X,Y,W,H`; from round 2, `--against`, item 4). The composition calls `layoutFrame(ctx, t, { format, hero: heroBox(camera, hero, W, H) })` last each frame, so the capture records the canvas text boxes in `layout.json` and `index.md`'s `## Layout`. For a person, add ~8 `snapshot --at` stills across the clip.
2. Spawn **one new subagent** (the Agent tool, a fresh agent: never a fork or a follow-up of the builder), model and effort from `state.mjs next`. Give it only:
   - the sheet paths, the strip sheets with `review/strips/index.md`, the crops and the full-res folder `review/strips/frames/`. It reads `index.md`'s `## Layout` first: a `FLAG` line (outside the safe area, over the hero, under the minimum size, caption over a label) is a finding to confirm on its still (Clarity or Composition P1; unreadable text P0); `Not recorded` frames are judged from the stills. It opens a full-res still only where a sheet or crop leaves a doubt and lists each under `Opened full-res:` in `QC-REVIEW.md` with the reason. A person keeps its ~8 stills,
   - `SPEC.md` (LOOK, MOTION, OBJECTS with their reference, BANNED), `storyboard.md` (check one still at every shot's action beat against its row) and the approved style frames,
   - `visual-direction.md` §11, §12, §13 and, for people, §1 "People are the hardest subject",
   - `audio/beats.json`,
   - this instruction: "Find defects. For each: the beat or time, what is wrong, the rubric test it fails (its name on the line: Reality, Clarity, Action, Figure-ground…), severity P0 (must fix: Set/Reality fail of the subject or the shot, slideshow (Action test), a hero merged into its ground (Figure-ground), wrong claim, unreadable text, broken render), P1 (should fix: craft, composition, transition, weak figure-ground separation, strobing on camera moves (Strobing), a section that fails Sound off, a fact repeated across layers (No repeat), a compared quantity shown only in text (Quantity), captions too high on 9:16 (Reels, TikTok: block top above ~70 % of the height; Shorts: block bottom above ~65 %; Composition), and a Set, Reality or Clarity defect smaller than a fail), P2 (nice to have). Write them to QC-REVIEW.md. Do not praise; do not fix."
3. A builder subagent fixes every P0 and P1, one per round (a new builder, never resumed, never the reviewer, model from `state.mjs next`, builder budget in `look-build.md`), given the round's P0/P1 list and the files (`look-build.md` "Parallel build"). It makes all fixes first, then one re-snapshot of what changed, runs `lint_scene.mjs` and `npx hyperframes check`, and returns per finding fixed / not fixed + one line, and the files touched. A fix-check subagent (`model: "sonnet"`) checks the new stills against the findings, pass/fail per item. Log the round in `PRODUCTION.md` (`state.mjs log`) and mark each item `FIXED` in `QC-REVIEW.md` (`score_run.mjs` counts open P0s).
4. Review again only if P0s, or Set, Reality or Clarity P1s, were open (`qc.md` Step 7); a lean test run stops after round 1; three rounds at most, then list what is open at Gate 4. Repeat rounds: `mv review/strips review/strips-<n>` (n = rounds done, never an existing name), then `node <skill-dir>/scripts/strips.mjs --against review/strips-<n> --targets <shot numbers or beat names>` (`--threshold <pct>`, default 1). Give the new reviewer `index.md` and only the `changed` frames: `changed (targeted)` is checked against its finding; `changed (not targeted)` is a new finding until shown intended; `unchanged` keeps its verdict; a targeted shot with no changed frame (`WARN`) means the fix did not show. The compare is a 64x36 grey heuristic: a tiny change can pass as `unchanged`.

## Sound-off quiz (Step 7, a second fresh subagent beside the reviewer)

- **`review/quiz.md`**, written at Gate 1: `| # | Beat | Question | Key |`, 5 rows: the mechanism; the key number with its comparison; the decision rule; a common misconception and its correction (the naive answer is the wrong one); one more claim. Each answerable from a headline, card or label alone, without the answer's words in the question. `Beat` = the name in `audio/beats.json` (keep names neutral). A claim changed after Gate 1: update `quiz.md` first.
- **Run** after `strips.mjs`, in every review round: a NEW subagent (never the builder or the reviewer). Give it ONLY the strip sheet paths, `review/strips/index.md` and the questions WITHOUT the Key column. Instruction: "Sound off, pictures only. Answer each question in one short sentence from what is on screen (headline, card, label, figure). If the frames do not show it, write 'not shown'. Do not use general knowledge; if you know the answer from elsewhere, still answer only what the frames show."
- **Score** (the main agent; a doubt is `partly correct`): `correct` = the claim with its number and comparison or correction where the Key has them; `partly correct` = the right idea missing those; `wrong` = a different claim, a contradiction or `not shown`. Write `review/quiz-result.md`: `# Quiz result`, a line `Round <n>`, then `| # | Beat | Question | Answer given | Key | Verdict |`. Each round overwrites it; `score_run.mjs` reads it (correct = 1, partly = 0.5).
- Each `wrong` becomes `P1 | <beat> t=<time> | Clarity | quiz Q<n> wrong: <what the frames fail to carry>` in `QC-REVIEW.md`, fixed like any Clarity P1 (`visual-direction.md` §12) and marked `FIXED`. Before calling a wrong answer on a long shot a defect, run `strips.mjs --per-shot 5`.

## Asset pass (before Gate 3, and again before the Step 6 blocking pass)

Catch asset defects (a part in the wrong place, garbled text, a shape that changes between shots) while a fix is one re-source or re-model:

1. One pair per drawn, modelled or found OBJECTS still beside its reference: `ffmpeg -loglevel error -y -i <still of x> -i review/ref-<x>.jpg -filter_complex "[0]scale=-1:960[a];[1]scale=-1:960[b];[a][b]hstack" review/pair-<x>.jpg`.
2. A fresh subagent (`state.mjs next`: asset pair check), given only the pair paths, SPEC OBJECTS and `visual-direction.md` §1 "Reality check": "For each pair: is the left the same kind of thing as the right — parts, where the action happens, proportions, materials; any garbled text or logo; does the same object match across pairs? P0 = Reality fail. List only defects."
3. Re-model or re-source every P0 before building on it. For an object modelled in code the reference is a build input (`look-build.md` Step 5): once the OBJECTS stills have landed, the media screening (mid tier, text only) picks 1–2 stills per object of each frame, normalises each (`ffmpeg -v error -y -i <still> -vf "scale=320:320:force_original_aspect_ratio=decrease,pad=320:320:(ow-iw)/2:(oh-ih)/2:white" review/refs-<frame>/<n>.jpg`, n = 1…8), writes the board (`ffmpeg -v error -y -start_number 1 -i review/refs-<frame>/%d.jpg -vf tile=4x2 -frames:v 1 review/refs-board-<frame>.jpg`, 1280 × 640) and returns the picks and a parts line per modelled object for SPEC OBJECTS (3–6 things: count, side, orientation, layer order). The style-frame author opens only the boards, once; full-size stills stay with the reviewer, who grades the parts lines too. A found CC0 model carries its shape: for it this is a check after.

## Benchmark review (Gate 3: the style frames against a reference board)

Measures taste at the cheap moment, separate from the defect review and the style-frame checker.

1. **The board.** The Brief's reference (Step 2): `measure/reference-board.jpg` from `measure_reference.sh` (two: `measure/<name>/`, both boards). No reference: `<skill-dir>/docs/assets/samples/` (Express stills for Express, `studio-*.jpg` for Studio, `latte.jpg` for object-studio). Boards stay local: never published, committed or posted.
2. **The reviewer.** A NEW subagent (never the style-frame checker or a builder), strongest model, given only the board path(s), the three style-frame stills, `style-frames/hook.mp4` and `SPEC.md`'s LOOK line: "Score the board and the style frames side by side, 1–5 each, on these rows: Detail density (parts per frame; markings legible at the macro), Material truth (each surface reads as its material), Light and shadow (contact shadows, ambient occlusion, separation), Text system (present, consistent, legible), Overlays caused by motion, Framing (subject fills the frame, text band clean, nothing clipped), Macro quality (sharpness, depth of field). Return only this table, `| Row | Reference | Style frames | Note |`, one line per row, then one line per row more than 1 point behind: what the board does that the frames do not. A row that does not apply to this look gets the same score on both sides and says why in Note."
3. **Record.** The main agent writes the table verbatim to `review/benchmark.md` (never opening the images) and logs `state.mjs log "5 Look | benchmark: <rows and scores> | <rows behind or ->"`.
4. **Rule.** A row more than 1 point below the board is a Gate 3 fix: fix the style frames, snapshot, a new reviewer scores again (log each round). `state.mjs approve 3` refuses while a row is behind, `review/benchmark.md` is missing or unreadable, or a style frame is newer. After two rounds still behind, pick a board closer to the look, log it, score once more.

## Model per job

Model and effort: `state.mjs next` (all: `state.mjs models`; with escalation).

## Autonomous mode

Every run is autonomous after the Brief (`qc.md` Step 7 ship rules). Lean review needs the user's own word.
