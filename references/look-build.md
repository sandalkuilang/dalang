# Steps 5–6: look, storyboard, style frames, build

## Step 5 — Look, storyboard, style frames → GATE 3

Read the chosen look's row in `looks.md`; `visual-direction.md` §1, the chosen energy's row in §3, §5–8; `craft-rules.md` §2–6; and `camera-motion.md` §1–4 and §8. Write:

- `SPEC.md` (template: `/hyperframes` `references/spec.md`): the LOOK line (the chosen look and energy in numbers — palette hex, faces and weights, light direction, grain, average shot length), the MOTION and ANCHORS lines (`camera-motion.md` §8), OBJECTS (each physical thing, its asset rung and source, and the real reference it is checked against), BANNED. Never skip it: without it every gap falls back to the generated default look.
- `storyboard.md`: the arc, a colour-script line and the axis per section (`camera-motion.md` §3), then the shot table, times from `beats.json`:

| # | Time | Beat | Job | Size · angle · lens | What we see (focal point; fg / mid / bg job) | Camera (+ what changed) | Continuous motion | Transition out (+ what fires it) | SFX (J/L) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.00–3.40 | `hook` | establish | CU · low · 85 mm | the hero (focal), sharp on a lit set; blurred rail fg; the hook word lands as the title | drift 1.00→1.04 | light sweeping across the hero | rack focus as the eye moves to the label | low whoosh, J −0.3 s |

Check: every OBJECTS item that is a physical thing has a real source (rungs 1–5) — a vector stand-in is a blocker, not a compromise; every shot where the subject acts has footage or 3D for it (§1 "Moving subjects"), and every person who moves or speaks has real or generated footage (§1 "People"); every beat visualises its claim, not just its words; continuous motion in every row; at Calm energy few hard cuts; the hero meets `visual-direction.md` §7's size; one hero move per shot; text in the format's safe zone and in the narration language; stylised characters (only if asked for) follow the mitten rule; a reference's numbers are used (Step 2). Shot grammar (`camera-motion.md`): arc named, Crack and Impact present; every row has a job; every perceptible camera move has a reason; each scene keeps its axis; sizes tighten toward the key beat and macro inserts have their own asset; a pause before the biggest cut and no two bursts touching; the final image is the payoff frame.

Then get the hero assets (resolve, ingest, cut out every OBJECTS item; free sources first). **Every file that lands in `assets/`** — images, clips, narration, music, SFX — goes into `ASSETS.json` at once (file, source URL or "generated"/"user", licence, author; model + prompt + seed for generated ones — format in `scripts/check_assets.mjs`).

**Style frames** (the look test; a change costs three frames now, every scene later): a small composition in `style-frames/src/` with the real assets and rig — the hook scene at its real timing from 0 s with the narration `<audio>`, then one explanation beat and the payoff as ~3 s clips. Load only the `/hyperframes` contract and `references/format.md` (plus `types/vertical.md` for 9:16), use the scaffold in §6. Snapshot the three at their mid points (`npx hyperframes snapshot --at <t1>,<t2>,<t3> --describe false`) into `style-frames/`, and render it once as the **moving sample** (`render --quality high --quiet --output style-frames/sample.mp4`, then `ffmpeg -t <hook end> -i style-frames/sample.mp4 -c copy style-frames/hook.mp4`) — stills can't show motion, rhythm or how the voice sits. Step 6 copies these scenes into `src/index.tpl.html`. Build nothing else before Gate 3. Show `storyboard.md`, `SPEC.md`, the three stills and the hook sample together and **wait for approval**.

## Step 6 — Build (hand off to /hyperframes)

1. Load `/hyperframes` and only the type guides this video needs (`types/explainer.md`, `types/character.md`, `types/data.md`, `types/3d.md`, plus `types/vertical.md` for 9:16). Answer its opening questions from `BRIEF.md`, `script.md`, `storyboard.md`, `SPEC.md` without re-asking the user — but **do not skip its design steps**: it follows the LOOK, OBJECTS and BANNED lines.
2. The approved style frames are the reference for every scene.
3. Search the catalog before building an effect; queries **in English** (others return nothing): `npx hyperframes catalog --query "<what the beat needs>"`, then `npx hyperframes add <name>`.
4. Load `audio/beats.js`; **every** animation time references `BEATS.<name>.start`, or `WORDS[i].start` for a change on a word that is not a beat. Never hard-code seconds. HTML attributes (`data-start`, `data-duration`) can't read JS: write them as `{{beat_name - 0.05}}` in `src/index.tpl.html` and run `node <skill-dir>/scripts/fill_times.mjs src/index.tpl.html index.html` after every template edit and every new take.
   Scaffold facts that each cost a lint round when missed: include GSAP yourself (`<script src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/gsap@3.14.2/dist/gsap.min.js"></script>`, before your timeline script); time a `<video>` **or** its wrapper, never both (a timed scene wrapper holds an untimed `<video>`, or the reverse); `media-treatment` adds `data-hf-id` to every tag, so edit the template with Edit, not fragile `sed` patterns; never put SVG inside a `<template>` (it renders empty), never `mix-blend-mode` on an SVG `<rect>` (it paints opaque); the same hero across a cut is a match cut, never a crossfade.
5. **Blocking pass (animatic):** every scene at final timing with rough motion. Render once at `--fps 10 --quality draft --quiet`, watch it against the narration — rhythm, shot variety, colour script — and run `stillness.mjs` on it now: too little motion is a structure problem (`visual-direction.md` §3 "Minimum motion"), cheap to fix here.
6. **Polish pass:** craft (`visual-direction.md` §4; physical timing, compositing and design in time from `camera-motion.md` §5–7), finish (§9: contact shadows, one light direction, grade and grain through `media-treatment`), depth and parallax (§6).
7. Audio: first normalise the narration (`loudness.mjs audio/narration.wav --fix <project>/assets/narration.m4a`) so the mix lands near −14 LUFS; music ducking under it (`/media-use` `references/mixing.md`; tempo from the energy); sound effects on key beats only, each `<audio>` with `data-duration` (without it a 0.4 s click "plays" to the end of the film). No music source and the Step 0 offer declined → deliver narration + SFX and say so.
8. `npx hyperframes lint` after each major section.
