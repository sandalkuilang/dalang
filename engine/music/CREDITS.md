# Bundled music beds

Free for any use, commercial included: public domain or CC0, read from each file's Wikimedia Commons page. Encoded by
DALANG as 64 kb/s mono MP3, the first 180 s at most, a 1.5 s fade-in, loudness −20 LUFS. `scaffold.mjs --music <mood>`
copies one into a film as `assets/music.mp3` and writes its ASSETS.json row.
