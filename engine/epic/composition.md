# Composition — layouts that don't look generated

Rounded cards in a symmetric grid on a flat ground is a dashboard, not an infographic. Before drawing, say where the
viewer stands (the canvas is a place: `references/express.md`); background, texture and data all belong to it. Pick ONE
pattern below per beat's last frame and name it in a comment; a card grid is at most one sub-zone (≤ ⅓ of the canvas).

## The patterns

1. **The Big Object**: one drawn object fills 40–70 % of the canvas; data lives on and around it (pinned labels, a
   chart built into it, callouts with leaders); it may bleed off an edge. Single-subject topics.
2. **The Bleed**: the hero (number, chart, object) cropped by the canvas edge, generous empty space opposite. One big
   value made enormous.
3. **Overlap stack**: 2–4 meaningful overlaps (number over the illustration, badge over a chart corner), each with a
   depth cue (hard shadow, outline, cut edge). Poster energy in square and story formats.
4. **Diagonal drive**: one diagonal (a rotated band, three steps down-right, a diagonal baseline), everything else calm.
   Growth, speed, decline.
5. **Editorial spread**: a hard 1/3 : 2/3 or 1/4 : 3/4 split; the thin column holds one towering element and the
   biggest type. Sober subjects.
6. **The specimen sheet**: ≥ 8 small drawn items in a strict labelled grid, a big plain title, one item may break the
   grid. "The 12 kinds of…", rankings, taxonomies.

## Tension rules (every pattern)

- One dense zone, one empty zone (≥ ~15 % of the canvas breathes).
- Three sizes at least; the largest ≥ 8× body text.
- Something crosses a boundary (its container, the canvas edge, a section line).
- The background is never uniform: a tinted shape, texture, ghosted number or paper gradient.
- Rotate at most one system of elements.

## Litmus

Could it hold a SaaS dashboard's data unchanged? Recompose. Cover the text: is the topic still clear from shapes? If
not, add the object. Do all four corners work equally? There is no focal point.
